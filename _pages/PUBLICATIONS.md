The Gao Lab studies precision environmental health and the epidemiology of aging. Our research asks how environmental exposures affect health in older adults, how biological aging contributes to these effects, and where prevention can improve health in later life.

1. **How does the environment affect health in older adults?** We use large population datasets to identify and quantify the environmental factors, sensitive exposure windows, and exposure settings that influence cardiovascular and brain health in older adults.
2. **How does the environment affect health through biological aging?** We integrate multi-omics and artificial intelligence to map the molecular pathways linking environmental exposures, biological aging, age-related disease, and healthy longevity. We identify biomarkers and develop tools to measure and track biological aging.
3. **How can we reduce environment-related aging risks?** We study how reducing harmful exposures, modifying individual behaviors, and targeting molecular pathways could promote healthy aging and inform public health strategies.

[Faculty profile at Peking University](https://sph.pku.edu.cn/info/1727/5358.htm)
