<p class="lab-intro">The Gao Lab studies precision environmental health and the epidemiology of aging. Our research asks how environmental exposures affect health in older adults, how biological aging mediates these effects, and how intervention can promote healthy aging.</p>

<ol class="lab-directions">
<li><strong>Which environmental exposures matter for health in later life?</strong> Using large population datasets, we identify and quantify the environmental factors, sensitive exposure windows, and exposure scenarios that shape cardiovascular and brain health in older adults.</li>
<li><strong>How does biological aging link exposure to disease?</strong> We integrate multi-omics and artificial intelligence to trace how environmental exposures accelerate biological aging, and how this acceleration in turn drives age-related disease or limits healthy longevity. Along the way, we develop biomarkers and tools to measure and track biological aging.</li>
<li><strong>How can these risks be reduced?</strong> We evaluate whether reducing harmful exposures, changing individual behaviors, and targeting molecular pathways can promote healthy aging, and translate the evidence into public health strategies.</li>
</ol>

<p class="lab-intro">Dr. Gao has published more than 80 papers and was named among the Stanford/Elsevier top 2% of scientists in 2025 and 2026. The lab leads the Guangxi Eco-Environmental Health and Aging Study (GEHAS) and maintains the open-source R package easyEWAS. <a href="https://sph.pku.edu.cn/info/1727/5358.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Faculty profile at Peking University</a></p>
